About the numbers

Nail sizes, thread lengths, reamers and catalogue lengths are from the maker's Fassier-Duval surgical technique, FD-ST-EN Rev M (Jan 2024): open the guide. The suggested size is this planner's rule (largest nail whose reamer passes the isthmus) and is yours to change. A drawing on a 2D film does not show rotation. Check every figure against the film and the set in theatre.
